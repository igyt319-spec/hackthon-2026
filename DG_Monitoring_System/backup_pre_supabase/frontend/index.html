Deterministic Rule: Post-Mains Restoration Shutdown Delays

Timestamp comparison: identifies avoidable fuel expenditure after grid mains was restored

Delay Threshold: 10 min

When the public utility grid returns online, automatic transfer switches (ATS) or plant operators should disconnect and cool down the generator within 5 to 10 minutes. Running generators beyond this threshold wastes costly diesel and creates unnecessary emissions. This rule-based verification is strictly deterministic and distinct from machine learning anomaly models.
